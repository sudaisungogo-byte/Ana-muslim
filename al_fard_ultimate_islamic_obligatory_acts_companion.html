The Foundations of Faith

The Five Pillars of Islam
(أركان الإسلام)

Islam is built upon five fundamental pillars. Fulfilling these obligatory acts (*Fard*) forms the bedrock of a Muslim's faith, spiritual discipline, and relationship with Allah (SWT).

1
Core Creed

Shahada

Declaration of Faith

Bearing witness that there is no god but Allah, and Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) is His final messenger.

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ ٱللَّٰهِ

2
5 Daily Prayers

Salah

Obligatory Worship

Performing the five daily prayers at prescribed times throughout the day and night.

3
Annual Alms

Zakat

Obligatory Charity

Giving 2.5% of qualifying accumulated wealth annually to purify wealth and help those in need.

4
Ramadan Fast

Sawm

Fasting Holy Month

Abstaining from food, drink, and desires from dawn to sunset during Ramadan for God-consciousness (*Taqwa*).

5
Pilgrimage

Hajj

Pilgrimage to Makkah

Obligatory once in a lifetime for every adult Muslim who is physically and financially capable.

Requires Ihram, Tawaf, & Arafat standing.

14 Essential Pillars of Prayer (Arkan)

If any pillar is omitted, the prayer is invalid and must be repeated.

  • 1. Qiyam: Standing during obligatory prayer for those able.
  • 2. Takbirat al-Ihram: The opening "Allahu Akbar".
  • 3. Reciting Surah Al-Fatiha: In every single Rakat.
  • 4. Ruku: Bowing until hands rest firmly on knees.
  • 5. Standing straight: Rising fully from bowing.
  • 6. Sujood: Prostrating on 7 limbs (forehead/nose, hands, knees, toes).
  • 7. Sitting between prostrations: Pausing upright between Sujood.
  • 8. Tumani'nah: Calmness and tranquility in every posture.

Final Tashahhud (At-Tahiyaat)

Recited in the sitting position of the final Rakat before Tasleem.

ٱلتَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّٰهِ وَٱلصَّلَوَاتُ وَٱلطَّيِّبَاتُ، ٱلسَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ ٱللَّٰهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، ٱلسَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَىٰ عِبَادِ ٱللَّٰهِ ٱلصَّالِحِينَ

"All compliments, prayers and pure deeds are for Allah. Peace be upon you, O Prophet, and the mercy of Allah and His blessings..."

Completion of Prayer (Tasleem): Conclude by turning head right then left saying: "ٱلسَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ ٱللَّٰهِ"

Purification Guides (Wudu & Ghusl)

Ritual purification is an absolute prerequisite condition (*Shart*) for Salah validity.

The 4 Obligatory (Fard) Acts of Wudu

1

Washing Entire Face

From hairline to bottom of chin, and earlobe to earlobe (Once obligatory, 3x Sunnah).

2

Washing Arms & Elbows

Washing both arms thoroughly including fingertips up to and including elbows.

3

Wiping Head

Wiping wet hands over head once (at least 1/4 of head or full head depending on school).

4

Washing Feet & Ankles

Washing both feet thoroughly up to and including ankles, cleaning between toes.

Fard Acts of Major Ritual Bath (Ghusl)

Ghusl is required after major ritual impurity (*Janabah*, post-menstruation, etc.).

1. Intention (Niyyah) Intending in heart to purify yourself for Allah's worship.
2. Rinse Mouth & Nose Rinsing mouth thoroughly and sniffing water into nostrils.
3. Wash Entire Body Ensuring water reaches every part of head, skin, and hair root.

Al-Fatiha (The Opening)

Obligation & Usage: Mandatory (*Fard*) in every single unit (Rakat) of Salah.

Virtue / Benefit: Known as Al-Shifa (The Cure) and Umm al-Kitab (Mother of the Book). Recited for healing and divine guidance.

Ayat al-Kursi (Throne Verse)

آية الكرسي

ٱللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْحَيُّ ٱلْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُۥ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ ۚ لَّهُۥ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلْأَرْضِ

Virtue / Benefit: Unmatched spiritual protection against evil eye, envy (*Hasad*), and whispered evil.
